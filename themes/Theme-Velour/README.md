# Velour

A velvet-lounge look for Stash v0.31+. Deep plum that reads as black until lit,
dusty rose for actions, champagne for small flourishes, and italic Instrument
Serif titles over Outfit.

- Cards are framed plum tiles: the cover sits inset with rounded corners and the
  text runs below it, lifting with a soft rose glow on hover.
- Controls are 10px rounded rectangles; main actions are rose pills.
- Covers the list toolbar, filter sidebar, selection toolbar, pagination, list,
  wall and tagger views, scene and detail pages, stats and settings, at desktop
  and phone widths.
- Fonts are bundled in `assets/fonts` (SIL OFL 1.1), so the theme makes no
  external requests.

Pure CSS; no change to Stash behaviour. Enable one theme at a time.
